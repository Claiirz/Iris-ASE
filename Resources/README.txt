X-Axis (Domain): Fixed from 0.0 to 1.0 (Godot Curve input sampling is always normalized 0 to 1).

0.0 = Level 1
0.5 = Level 50
1.0 = Level 100

Y-Axis (Min Value & Max Value): Represents your Stat Multiplier.

Quick Rule of Thumb for Stat Curves
Y = 1.0 at X = 0.0 (Level 1): Represents 100% of your base stats.
Y = 2.0: Doubles your base stats.
Y = 10.0: 10x your base stats.
If you want the base health in the Inspector to be the exact starting health at Level 1, 
always start your curves at Y = 1.0.
